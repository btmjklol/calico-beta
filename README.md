# Calico (test version)

Calico is a practice partner that listens to you play and gives you one small thing to practice at a time. It's for the hours between lessons, not instead of them. Guitar, bass, ukulele, mandolin, violin, keys, drums, flute, recorder, clarinet, sax, trumpet or your voice. With sheet music, scales in every key, and music theory you can answer by playing. It hears what you play through your computer's microphone and moves on when it hears you do it.

This is an early test copy for Windows. Thanks for trying it.

## Get it

Go to **[Releases](../../releases/latest)** and pick the file for your computer:

| Your computer | Download | Then |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Windows | `Calico-0.4.0-setup.exe` (installs) or `Calico-0.4.0-portable.exe` (just runs) | If Windows says it protected your PC: **More info**, then **Run anyway**. |
| Mac with Apple chip (M1 or newer) | `Calico-0.4.0-mac-arm64.dmg` | Drag Calico to Applications. The first time, **right-click it, choose Open**, then Open again. |
| Older Intel Mac | `Calico-0.4.0-mac-x64.dmg` | Same as above. |
| Linux | `Calico-0.4.0-linux-x86_64.AppImage` (any Linux) or `Calico-0.4.0-linux-amd64.deb` (Ubuntu, Debian) | Make the AppImage executable, then run it. |
| Android phone or tablet | `Calico-0.4.0-android.apk` (open this page on the phone) | Tap the file. If it asks, allow installs from your browser, then **Install**. |
| iPhone, iPad or Chromebook | Open **bigtimemachine.org/calico/** | iPhone: Safari, Share, **Add to Home Screen**. A real iPhone app comes once there's an Apple developer account. |

The warnings are because the test copies aren't signed yet. When it asks for the microphone, say yes. Nothing you play leaves your device.

## What to try

- Pick your instrument at the top right. If you play more than one, try a second: things you already know carry over.
- Press **Let Calico listen** and do what the screen asks.
- Click **I can already do this** to skip ahead to where you are.
- On drums, press **Count me in** and play along with the line.
- Leave it alone for a minute and watch.
- Under **Tuner & Metronome**, start the band and strum: drums and bass follow your tempo and your chords.

Click **[Issues](../../issues/new)** and say what you did and what went wrong (or what you liked). A screenshot helps. Things worth reporting:

- it didn't hear something you played right, or it heard something you didn't play
- a tip that was wrong or confusing
- anything that felt too hard, too easy, or boring
- crashes, freezes, anything that looked broken
